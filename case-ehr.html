EHR Workflow Design

Multi-role healthcare interface concepts for doctors, nurses, lab technicians, admins and patients across clinical workflows.

Problem / Challenge

Clinical systems must support different roles without overwhelming users. The design challenge was to create usable dashboards and workflows for high-pressure healthcare contexts.

Constraints / Context

  • Multiple personas and permission levels
  • Clinical environments with time pressure
  • Need for low-light/dark mode considerations in ICU contexts
  • Limited direct access to proprietary systems

Outcome

  • Created multi-persona UX concepts for healthcare workflows.
  • Converted research findings into role-based screens and patterns.
  • Strengthened domain credibility across enterprise healthcare UX.

Learnings

  • Complex UX needs clear states, constraints and prioritization.
  • Scannable structure matters as much as visual polish.
  • Reusable patterns improve handoff and future iteration.
